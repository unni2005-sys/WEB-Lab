Welcome to the Department of Computer Applications 

        
            The Department of Computer Applications at the
            Adi Shankara Institute of Engineering and Technology (ASIET)
            in Kalady, Kerala, offers a full-time, two-year
            Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program approved by
            the AICTE and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University.

About the Department 

        
            The department focuses on providing students with strong theoretical knowledge and practical skills in computer
            applications. Students are encouraged to develop their  programming, problem-solving and technical skills through
            academic activities and practical sessions.
